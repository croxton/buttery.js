Seasons

Four parallax bands, each built from layers that scroll at different speeds. There is no JavaScript setup on this page: every layer is a data-buttery attribute.

Scroll down ↓

Spring

Spring

Petals let go before the leaves arrive. The sky lags at half your scroll speed, the far hills a little less, the blossom trees almost keep up with the page, and the petals overtake everything.

<section class="band" id="spring">
  <div class="band__sky"
    data-buttery="from: top-bottom; to: bottom-top; elem: #spring"
    data-buttery-translate="0 -50vh | 0 50vh">…</div>
  <div data-buttery="…"
    data-buttery-translate="0 -30vh | 0 30vh">far hills</div>
  <div data-buttery="…"
    data-buttery-translate="0 -10vh | 0 10vh">trees</div>
  <div data-buttery="…"
    data-buttery-translate="-4vw 30vh | 4vw -50vh">petals</div>
</section>

Every layer measures the band (elem: #spring), which never moves, so the ranges stay accurate however far a layer travels.

Summer

Summer

Long light, short shadows. The further back a layer is, the more it lags behind the page. The near clouds go the other way: their range runs upwards, so they rush past faster than you scroll.

<div class="band__particles"
  data-buttery="from: top-bottom; to: bottom-top; elem: #summer"
  data-buttery-translate="0 40vh | 0 -60vh">…</div>

Autumn

Autumn

The petals are leaves now. Their layer has an -md override, so from the md breakpoint up they fall further and drift sideways more. Resize the window to see it switch.

<div class="band__particles"
  data-buttery="from: top-bottom; to: bottom-top; elem: #autumn"
  data-buttery-translate="-4vw 30vh | 4vw -50vh"
  data-buttery-translate-md="-12vw 50vh | 12vw -90vh">…</div>

Winter

Winter

Snow on the pines, falling while you scroll down. The band takes 200vh of scrolling to pass, and the snow layer travels 240vh downwards in that time, so it moves down the screen instead of up. Each title rises against the scroll and fades in and out with a via value, so it's fully visible only when its band fills the screen.

<h2 class="band__title"
  data-buttery="from: top-bottom; to: bottom-top; elem: #winter"
  data-buttery-translate="0 20vh | 0 -20vh"
  data-buttery-opacity="0 | 1 | 0">Winter</h2>
